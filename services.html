Coldroom systems

Coldroom Installation, Service & Repairs

Design, installation, servicing and repairs for chilled and frozen coldrooms used by food, medicine, seafood, hotels and retail businesses.

  • Insulated panels, doors and flooring
  • Evaporators, condensing units and controls
  • Fault diagnosis, leak testing and temperature checks
Industrial cooling

Industrial Chillers Installation, Service & Repairs

Heavy-duty chiller installation, maintenance and repair for factories, processing plants and commercial cooling operations.

  • Water-cooled and air-cooled chillers
  • Compressor, pump and control repairs
  • Refrigerant charging and performance testing
Comfort cooling

Airconditions Installation, Service & Repairs

Professional installation, routine servicing and repairs for residential, office, shop, school and commercial airconditioning systems.

  • Split, cassette and package units
  • Gas charging, leak checks and coil cleaning
  • Airflow, drainage and electrical fault repairs
Critical cold storage

Mortuary Coldroom Installation, Service & Repairs

Reliable mortuary coldroom setup, servicing and fault repairs for facilities that require stable low-temperature storage.

  • Low-temperature room design and setup
  • Temperature stability and safety controls
  • Emergency repairs and maintenance support
Marine freezing

Vessel Cold Freezers Installation, Service & Repairs

Cold freezer installation, servicing and repairs for vessels, marine storage and fishing operations that depend on reliable freezing.

  • Marine freezer installation and repair
  • Compressor, controls and pipework service
  • Load testing and cooling performance checks
